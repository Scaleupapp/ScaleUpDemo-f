## App Store Description (iOS)

**Title:** ScaleUp – English for Work

**Subtitle:** AI-powered fluency, built for you

**Description:**

ScaleUp finds your real English level in 9 minutes — then builds a personalized learning plan around exactly what you need.

No generic lessons. No wasted time on things you already know.

**How it works:**
1. Tell us your role and goal (2 min)
2. Rate yourself honestly across speaking, writing, listening, and reading (1 min)
3. Complete a 9-question adaptive diagnostic — reading, listening, and speaking (6 min)
4. Get your ScaleUp Score, a gap breakdown, and your Day-1 plan instantly

**What you get:**
• Your true proficiency level — not what you guessed
• A ranked list of your highest-impact gaps
• A personalized daily plan calibrated to your job role
• Progress recalibration every 30 days so your plan evolves as you do

ScaleUp is built for working professionals who need real English fluency — not test prep.

## Play Store Description (Android)

**Title:** ScaleUp – English for Work

**Short description:** Know your real English level. Get a plan. Start improving today.

**Full description:**

Most English apps make you start from scratch. ScaleUp starts where you actually are.

In 9 minutes, ScaleUp's adaptive diagnostic finds your true proficiency level — then builds you a personalized learning plan targeting only your real gaps.

**The Day-1 Diagnostic:**
→ 3 reading questions (adaptive difficulty)
→ 3 listening questions (real workplace audio)
→ 3 speaking prompts (AI-scored, instant feedback)

**Your results include:**
→ A ScaleUp Score (0–100 scale, mapped to CEFR A1–C2)
→ A gap breakdown across all 4 skills
→ A personalized daily plan matched to your role
→ Recalibration reminders at day 30 and day 45

No fluff. No streaks for the sake of streaks. Just the fastest path from where you are to where you need to be.

ScaleUp is for professionals who need business English — for meetings, emails, presentations, and client calls.
