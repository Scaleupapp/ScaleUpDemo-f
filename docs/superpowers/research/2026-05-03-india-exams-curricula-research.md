# India Exams + Academic Curricula Research — 2026 and Beyond

**Research date:** 2026-05-03
**Purpose:** Seed ScaleUp's `exam_preparation` and `academic_excellence` topic taxonomies. Each exam entry includes the testable subject/section list intended to drive diagnostic topic generation. India is the primary lens throughout.

**Methodology note:** Verified with conducting-body sites (UPSC, NTA, NMC, ICAI, IBO, CISCE, CBSE, Consortium of NLUs, IIT Guwahati for GATE, mba.com, IBPS) and corroborated against major Indian prep platforms (Careers360, Shiksha, Adda247, Vajiram & Ravi, Vedantu, Allen, PW). Aspirant volumes are best-estimate ranges based on most recent NTA/conducting-body data; treat as order-of-magnitude.

## Section A: Top 50 Exams (grouped by category)

### Category 1: Civil Services & Defense

#### 1. UPSC Civil Services Examination (UPSC CSE)
- **Conducting body:** Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree from a recognised university; age 21–32 (general; relaxations for OBC/SC/ST/PwBD); attempt limits 6/9/unlimited by category
- **Frequency:** Annual (Prelims May, Mains August, Personality Test Jan–Apr next year)
- **Sections:**
  - **Prelims:** GS Paper 1 (objective), CSAT Paper 2 (objective, qualifying)
  - **Mains:** Essay, GS-I, GS-II, GS-III, GS-IV (Ethics), Optional Paper-I, Optional Paper-II, Compulsory Indian Language (qualifying), English (qualifying)
  - **Personality Test (Interview)**
- **Annual aspirants:** ~10–13 lakh apply; ~5–6 lakh appear
- **Career outcome:** IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS, IAAS and other Group A/B central services (~900–1,000 vacancies)
- **Topic taxonomy:** Indian History (Ancient/Medieval/Modern), Art & Culture, Indian & World Geography, Indian Polity & Governance, Indian Constitution, Economy (incl. Budget, Banking, External Sector), Environment & Ecology, Science & Technology, Current Affairs, International Relations, Internal Security, Disaster Management, Ethics/Integrity/Aptitude, Comprehension, Logical Reasoning & Analytical Ability, Decision Making, General Mental Ability, Basic Numeracy, Data Interpretation; plus Optional subject (one of 48 — e.g., PSIR, Sociology, Geography, Public Administration, Anthropology, History, Mathematics, Literature of an Indian language, etc.)

#### 2. UPSC Engineering Services Examination (UPSC ESE / IES)
- **Conducting body:** UPSC
- **Eligibility:** Engineering degree (CE/ME/EE/ECE); age 21–30
- **Frequency:** Annual (Prelims Feb, Mains June)
- **Sections:** Prelims (GS & Engineering Aptitude + Engineering Discipline Paper), Mains (two discipline papers), Personality Test
- **Annual aspirants:** ~2–3 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Group A engineering posts in CPWD, Railways, Telecom, Border Roads, Indian Defence Service of Engineers, etc.
- **Topic taxonomy:** General Studies & Engineering Aptitude (current affairs, ethics, project management, ICT, environment, design-drawing, engineering maths); discipline-specific (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics & Telecom) full UG syllabus

#### 3. UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant
- **Conducting body:** UPSC
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree; age 20–25
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:** Paper-I (General Ability & Intelligence), Paper-II (General Studies, Essay & Comprehension), Physical Standards Test, Interview
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3–4 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Assistant Commandant in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB
- **Topic taxonomy:** General Mental Ability, GK & Current Events, History, Polity & Economy, Geography, Environment, Science & Tech, Essay Writing, Comprehension, Precis

#### 4. National Defence Academy & Naval Academy Examination (NDA & NA)
- **Conducting body:** UPSC
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 pass (PCM for Air Force/Navy/Naval Academy; any stream for Army); unmarried; age 16.5–19.5
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (NDA-I and NDA-II)
- **Sections:** Mathematics (300 marks), General Ability Test (English + GK = 600 marks), SSB Interview (900 marks)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~5–6 lakh per attempt
- **Career outcome:** Cadet at NDA Khadakwasla → commission as officer in Army/Navy/Air Force
- **Topic taxonomy:** Algebra, Matrices & Determinants, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry 2D/3D, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations, Vector Algebra, Statistics & Probability; English (grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, cohesion); Physics, Chemistry, General Science, History, Geography, Current Events

#### 5. Combined Defence Services Examination (CDS)
- **Conducting body:** UPSC
- **Eligibility:** Graduate (engineering for AFA, BSc/BA for OTA/IMA/INA); unmarried (relaxation for OTA women); age 19–25
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (CDS-I Feb, CDS-II Sep)
- **Sections:** English, General Knowledge, Elementary Mathematics (not for OTA), SSB Interview
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3–5 lakh per attempt
- **Career outcome:** Officer at IMA Dehradun, INA Ezhimala, AFA Hyderabad, OTA Chennai
- **Topic taxonomy:** English (spotting errors, fill in the blanks, synonyms/antonyms, comprehension); GK (current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, science); Elementary Math (Class 10 level — arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics)

#### 6. Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT)
- **Conducting body:** Indian Air Force
- **Eligibility:** Graduate (PCM for Flying); age 20–24 (Flying), 20–26 (Ground Duty)
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (AFCAT-1 Feb, AFCAT-2 Aug)
- **Sections:** General Awareness, Verbal Ability in English, Numerical Ability, Reasoning & Military Aptitude Test; EKT (Engineering Knowledge Test) for technical branches
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3–4 lakh per attempt
- **Career outcome:** Flying Officer / Ground Duty Officer in IAF
- **Topic taxonomy:** English (comprehension, error detection, synonyms/antonyms, cloze, idioms); GK (history, geography, sports, polity, economy, defence, current affairs); Numerical (decimals, fractions, simplification, average, profit/loss, ratio, percentage, time/work/distance); Reasoning (verbal & spatial)

#### 7. Indian Navy Entrance Test (INET) / SSC Officer
- **Conducting body:** Indian Navy
- **Eligibility:** Graduate/Engineering (varies by branch); age 21–24/25
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly
- **Sections:** English, Reasoning & Numerical Ability, GK, General Science & Mathematical Aptitude
- **Annual aspirants:** ~50,000–80,000
- **Career outcome:** Sub-Lieutenant in Indian Navy (Executive, Education, Technical, Logistics branches)
- **Topic taxonomy:** English, Reasoning, GK & Current Affairs, General Science, Mathematical Aptitude

#### 8. State Public Service Commission Exams (BPSC, UPPSC, MPPSC, MPSC, TNPSC, KPSC, RPSC, APSC, WBPSC, etc.)
- **Conducting body:** State Public Service Commissions
- **Eligibility:** Graduate; age varies (typically 21–40 with state-specific relaxations)
- **Frequency:** Annual (varies by state)
- **Sections:** Prelims (GS + State-specific GK + CSAT), Mains (descriptive — GS papers + Optional + Language papers), Interview
- **Annual aspirants:** UPPSC ~6–8 lakh, BPSC ~4–5 lakh, MPPSC ~3–4 lakh, MPSC ~3–4 lakh, TNPSC ~5–8 lakh, others ~1–3 lakh each
- **Career outcome:** State civil services (Deputy Collector, DSP, BDO, state secretariat services, etc.)
- **Topic taxonomy:** Indian History (with state-specific history emphasis), Geography (India + state), Polity & Governance, Economy, Environment, Science & Tech, Current Affairs (national + state), State-specific GK (geography, history, culture, art, demographics, schemes), CSAT (comprehension, reasoning, basic numeracy), Regional Language (Marathi/Tamil/Hindi/Kannada/Bengali/Telugu/etc. — typically qualifying)

### Category 2: Engineering Entrance

#### 9. Joint Entrance Examination — Main (JEE Main)
- **Conducting body:** National Testing Agency (NTA)
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 pass with PCM (75% or top 20 percentile for NIT/IIIT/GFTI); age — no upper limit
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (Session 1 Jan, Session 2 Apr)
- **Sections:** Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics; Paper 2A (B.Arch) — Math + Aptitude + Drawing; Paper 2B (B.Planning) — Math + Aptitude + Planning. Each subject has Section A (MCQ) + Section B (numerical value)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~14–15 lakh unique candidates
- **Career outcome:** Admission to NITs, IIITs, GFTIs and qualifier for JEE Advanced
- **Topic taxonomy (Paper 1):**
  - **Physics:** Units & Measurements, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work-Energy-Power, Rotational Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Solids & Liquids, Thermodynamics, Kinetic Theory of Gases, Oscillations & Waves, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current & Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction & AC, Electromagnetic Waves, Optics, Dual Nature of Matter & Radiation, Atoms & Nuclei, Electronic Devices, Experimental Skills
  - **Chemistry:** Physical (Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Solutions, Equilibrium, Redox & Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics); Inorganic (Periodic Table, p/d/f-block, Coordination Compounds, Metallurgy); Organic (Basic Concepts, Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes/arenes, Alcohols/Phenols/Ethers, Aldehydes/Ketones/Acids, Amines, Biomolecules, Polymers, Chemistry in Everyday Life)
  - **Mathematics:** Sets/Relations/Functions, Complex Numbers, Matrices & Determinants, Permutations & Combinations, Binomial Theorem, Sequences & Series, Limits/Continuity/Differentiability, Integral Calculus, Differential Equations, Coordinate Geometry, 3D Geometry, Vector Algebra, Statistics & Probability, Trigonometry

#### 10. JEE Advanced
- **Conducting body:** Rotating IIT (organising institute changes annually under JAB)
- **Eligibility:** Top ~2.5 lakh JEE Main qualifiers; age ≤ 25 (general); max 2 attempts in consecutive years
- **Frequency:** Annual (May–June)
- **Sections:** Paper 1 + Paper 2 (each has Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics; mix of MCQ-single, MCQ-multi, numerical value, matching)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~1.8–2.2 lakh appear
- **Career outcome:** Admission to 23 IITs and IISc/IISERs (via separate counselling)
- **Topic taxonomy:** Same broad subjects as JEE Main but deeper — adds topics like Thermal Physics, Modern Physics extensions, Inorganic qualitative analysis, more demanding Calculus & Coordinate Geometry, Vectors & 3D

#### 11. BITSAT (Birla Institute of Technology and Science Admission Test)
- **Conducting body:** BITS Pilani
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with PCM/PCB, ≥75% aggregate
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (May–June)
- **Sections:** Physics, Chemistry, English Proficiency, Logical Reasoning, Mathematics/Biology
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3 lakh
- **Career outcome:** B.E./B.Pharm at BITS Pilani/Goa/Hyderabad
- **Topic taxonomy:** Class 11–12 PCM/B (NCERT-aligned), English (grammar, vocabulary, comprehension), Logical Reasoning (verbal + non-verbal)

#### 12. VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination)
- **Conducting body:** Vellore Institute of Technology
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with PCM/PCB, ≥60%
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:** Mathematics OR Biology (40 Q), Physics (35 Q), Chemistry (35 Q), Aptitude (10 Q), English (5 Q)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3.5 lakh
- **Career outcome:** B.Tech at VIT Vellore/Chennai/AP/Bhopal
- **Topic taxonomy:** Class 11–12 NCERT PCM/B, Quantitative & Analytical Aptitude, English usage

#### 13. SRMJEEE / MET (Manipal) / COMEDK UGET / KCET / MHT-CET / WBJEE / AP/TS EAMCET / KEAM
- **Conducting body:** Respective universities/state authorities (state CETs run by their state CET cells)
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with PCM/PCB
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:** Physics, Chemistry, Math (and Biology for medical stream); some include English/Aptitude
- **Annual aspirants:** Combined across state CETs ~15–20 lakh (MHT-CET ~5L, KCET ~3L, WBJEE ~1L, EAMCET ~3–4L, KEAM ~1L, COMEDK ~1L, MET/SRMJEEE/Amrita each ~1–3L)
- **Career outcome:** Admission to state engineering colleges or named private universities
- **Topic taxonomy:** Class 11–12 NCERT/state-board PCM(B); state CETs follow state-board syllabus

### Category 3: Medical Entrance

#### 14. NEET UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test — Undergraduate)
- **Conducting body:** NTA on behalf of NMC
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 with PCB/PCBE, ≥50% (40% reserved); age ≥17
- **Frequency:** Annual (May)
- **Sections:** Physics (45 Q), Chemistry (45 Q), Botany (45 Q), Zoology (45 Q) — total 180 Q in 180 minutes
- **Annual aspirants:** ~22–24 lakh
- **Career outcome:** MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BSMS, BUMS, BVSc admission across India
- **Topic taxonomy:**
  - **Physics (20 units):** Physical World & Measurement, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work/Energy/Power, Motion of System of Particles & Rigid Body, Gravitation, Properties of Bulk Matter, Thermodynamics, Behaviour of Perfect Gas & Kinetic Theory, Oscillations & Waves, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects & Magnetism, EMI & AC, EM Waves, Optics, Dual Nature, Atoms & Nuclei, Electronic Devices, Experimental Skills
  - **Chemistry (20 units):** Some Basic Concepts, Structure of Atom, Classification & Periodicity, Chemical Bonding, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Redox, p-block, Organic Chemistry: Basic Principles, Hydrocarbons, Solutions, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, d & f-block, Coordination Compounds, Haloalkanes/arenes, Alcohols/Phenols/Ethers, Aldehydes/Ketones/Carboxylic Acids, Amines, Biomolecules
  - **Biology (10 units; Botany + Zoology):** Diversity in Living World, Structural Organisation in Animals & Plants, Cell Structure & Function, Plant Physiology, Human Physiology, Reproduction, Genetics & Evolution, Biology & Human Welfare, Biotechnology & Its Applications, Ecology & Environment

#### 15. NEET PG
- **Conducting body:** National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)
- **Eligibility:** MBBS with completed internship; valid registration
- **Frequency:** Annual (Aug)
- **Sections:** Single computer-based test, 200 MCQs covering full MBBS curriculum
- **Annual aspirants:** ~2.1–2.4 lakh
- **Career outcome:** MD/MS/PG Diploma seats across India
- **Topic taxonomy:** Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Forensic Medicine, Community Medicine (PSM), Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Orthopaedics, Anaesthesia, Radiology, ENT, Ophthalmology, Dermatology & Venereology, Psychiatry

#### 16. INI-CET (Institute of National Importance Combined Entrance Test)
- **Conducting body:** AIIMS New Delhi (for AIIMS, JIPMER, PGIMER, NIMHANS, SCTIMST)
- **Eligibility:** MBBS with completed internship
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (Jan, Jul)
- **Sections:** 200 MCQs across pre/para/clinical subjects
- **Annual aspirants:** ~12,000–18,000 per session
- **Career outcome:** PG seats at INIs (very limited, highly competitive)
- **Topic taxonomy:** Same MBBS subject list as NEET PG, with greater emphasis on recent advances and AIIMS-style questions

#### 17. NEET MDS
- **Conducting body:** NBEMS
- **Eligibility:** BDS with completed internship
- **Frequency:** Annual (May)
- **Sections:** 240 MCQs covering BDS curriculum
- **Annual aspirants:** ~25,000–30,000
- **Career outcome:** MDS admission across India
- **Topic taxonomy:** General Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Dental Materials, General & Dental Pharmacology, General Pathology & Microbiology, General Medicine & Surgery, Oral Pathology & Microbiology, Oral Medicine & Radiology, Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics, Periodontology, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Orthodontics, Prosthodontics, Paedodontics, Public Health Dentistry

#### 18. FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduates Examination)
- **Conducting body:** NBEMS
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizens / OCI with primary medical qualification from foreign institution
- **Frequency:** Twice yearly (Jun, Dec)
- **Sections:** 300 MCQs (two papers) covering MBBS subjects
- **Annual aspirants:** ~30,000–45,000
- **Career outcome:** Permanent registration with State Medical Council to practice in India
- **Topic taxonomy:** Pre-clinical (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry), Para-clinical (Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Forensic Medicine, Community Medicine), Clinical (Medicine, Surgery, OBG, Paediatrics, Ophthalmology, ENT, Orthopaedics, Anaesthesia, Radiology, Dermatology, Psychiatry)

### Category 4: Management / MBA

#### 19. Common Admission Test (CAT)
- **Conducting body:** Indian Institutes of Management (rotating IIM convenor)
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's with ≥50% (45% reserved); final-year students eligible
- **Frequency:** Annual (Nov–Dec)
- **Sections:** VARC (24 Q), DILR (22 Q), QA (22 Q) — 40 min each, 120 min total, 68 Q
- **Annual aspirants:** ~3.3–3.5 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Admission to 21 IIMs + many top B-schools (FMS, MDI, SPJIMR, IIT MBA, etc.)
- **Topic taxonomy:**
  - **VARC:** Reading Comprehension, Para Jumbles, Para Summary, Odd Sentence Out, Sentence Completion/Inference, Vocabulary
  - **DILR:** Tables, Bar/Line/Pie Charts, Caselets, Venn Diagrams, Linear & Circular Arrangement, Cube/Matrix Puzzles, Games & Tournaments, Logical Connectives
  - **QA:** Number System, Arithmetic (Percentages, Ratio, Avg, SI/CI, Profit/Loss, TSD, Time & Work, Mixtures), Algebra (Equations, Inequalities, Functions, Logarithms, Progressions), Geometry & Mensuration, Modern Math (P&C, Probability, Set Theory)

#### 20. XAT (Xavier Aptitude Test)
- **Conducting body:** XLRI Jamshedpur
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree
- **Frequency:** Annual (Jan)
- **Sections:** Verbal & Logical Ability, Decision Making, Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation, General Knowledge
- **Annual aspirants:** ~1 lakh
- **Career outcome:** XLRI, XIMB, GIM, IMT and ~160 other B-schools
- **Topic taxonomy:** VA & LR (RC, vocab, grammar, critical reasoning, para completion); Decision Making (ethical/business case scenarios); QA & DI (full quant + DI sets); GK (current affairs, business, history, geography, sports)

#### 21. CMAT (Common Management Admission Test)
- **Conducting body:** NTA (for AICTE)
- **Eligibility:** Graduate
- **Frequency:** Annual (May)
- **Sections:** Quantitative Techniques & Data Interpretation, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension, General Awareness, Innovation & Entrepreneurship (optional)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~75,000–1 lakh
- **Career outcome:** AICTE-approved MBA programs (JBIMS, SIMSREE, GIM, Welingkar, etc.)
- **Topic taxonomy:** Quant & DI, Logical Reasoning, English/Comprehension, GK & Current Affairs, Innovation & Entrepreneurship

#### 22. MAT (Management Aptitude Test)
- **Conducting body:** All India Management Association (AIMA)
- **Eligibility:** Graduate
- **Frequency:** Quarterly (Feb, May, Sep, Dec)
- **Sections:** Language Comprehension, Mathematical Skills, Data Analysis & Sufficiency, Intelligence & Critical Reasoning, Indian & Global Environment
- **Annual aspirants:** ~2 lakh (cumulative across cycles)
- **Career outcome:** ~600 MBA institutes accept MAT
- **Topic taxonomy:** Language Comprehension, Math, DI/DS, Reasoning, GK/Current Affairs

#### 23. SNAP (Symbiosis National Aptitude Test)
- **Conducting body:** Symbiosis International University
- **Eligibility:** Graduate with ≥50%
- **Frequency:** Up to 3 attempts annually (Dec)
- **Sections:** General English (RC, verbal reasoning, verbal ability), Quantitative-DI-Data Sufficiency, Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- **Annual aspirants:** ~80,000
- **Career outcome:** SIBM, SCMHRD, SIIB, etc.
- **Topic taxonomy:** English, Quant + DI + DS, Analytical & Logical Reasoning

#### 24. NMAT by GMAC (now by NMIMS)
- **Conducting body:** Graduate Management Admission Council
- **Eligibility:** Graduate
- **Frequency:** 75-day window (Oct–Dec); up to 3 attempts
- **Sections:** Language Skills, Quantitative Skills, Logical Reasoning
- **Annual aspirants:** ~75,000
- **Career outcome:** NMIMS Mumbai, ISB Hyderabad PGP Pro, VIT, SPJIMR PGMPW
- **Topic taxonomy:** Language Skills, Quantitative Skills (incl. DI/DS), Logical Reasoning

#### 25. IIFT MBA Entrance
- **Conducting body:** NTA (for IIFT)
- **Eligibility:** Graduate
- **Frequency:** Annual (Dec)
- **Sections:** Verbal Ability & RC, Quantitative Analysis, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, GK
- **Annual aspirants:** ~50,000–60,000
- **Career outcome:** IIFT Delhi/Kolkata/Kakinada (MBA International Business)
- **Topic taxonomy:** VA/RC, QA, DI/LR, GK (heavy on international trade & current affairs)

#### 26. TISSNET
- **Conducting body:** Tata Institute of Social Sciences
- **Eligibility:** Graduate
- **Frequency:** Annual (Feb)
- **Sections:** General Awareness, English Proficiency, Mathematics & Logical Reasoning
- **Annual aspirants:** ~30,000
- **Career outcome:** TISS Mumbai/Tuljapur/Hyderabad/Guwahati MA programs (HRM&LR, Social Work, etc.)
- **Topic taxonomy:** GK & Social Awareness (heavy on social sector, policy), English, Math & Logical Reasoning

### Category 5: Common University

#### 27. CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test — Undergraduate)
- **Conducting body:** NTA
- **Eligibility:** Class 12 pass
- **Frequency:** Annual (May)
- **Sections:** Section 1 (Languages: 13 in 1A, 20 in 1B); Section 2 (Domain Subjects: 23 options); Section 3 (General Test). Candidate picks up to 5 subjects total
- **Annual aspirants:** ~13–15 lakh
- **Career outcome:** UG admission to ~250+ central/state/private universities (DU, JNU, BHU, Allahabad, Jamia, Hyderabad, etc.)
- **Topic taxonomy:** All based on NCERT Class 12 syllabus —
  - **Domains:** Accountancy, Agriculture, Anthropology, Biology, Business Studies, Chemistry, Computer Science/IT, Economics, Engineering Graphics, Entrepreneurship, Environmental Studies, Fine Arts, Geography, History, Home Science, Knowledge Tradition–India, Legal Studies, Mass Media, Mathematics, Performing Arts, Physical Education, Physics, Political Science, Psychology, Sanskrit, Sociology, Teaching Aptitude
  - **Languages:** Hindi, English, and 20+ Indian/Foreign languages (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Urdu, Sanskrit, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, etc.)
  - **General Test:** GK, Current Affairs, General Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, Quantitative Reasoning, Logical Reasoning

#### 28. CUET PG
- **Conducting body:** NTA
- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree
- **Frequency:** Annual (Mar–Apr)
- **Sections:** Subject-specific paper (~75 questions) — choose from 150+ subjects
- **Annual aspirants:** ~6–8 lakh
- **Career outcome:** PG admission across central + participating universities
- **Topic taxonomy:** Subject-specific UG curriculum (one of 150+ subjects, e.g., MA English, MSc Physics, MSc Math, MA Economics, MA History, MCom, MSW, MA Psychology, etc.)

### Category 6: Banking / Insurance

#### 29. IBPS PO / SBI PO (Probationary Officer)
- **Conducting body:** Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) / State Bank of India
- **Eligibility:** Graduate; age 20–30
- **Frequency:** Annual (separate notifications)
- **Sections:**
  - **Prelims:** English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability
  - **Mains:** Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, English Language, Data Analysis & Interpretation, Descriptive (Letter + Essay)
  - Interview
- **Annual aspirants:** IBPS PO ~10–12 lakh, SBI PO ~8–10 lakh
- **Career outcome:** PO/Junior Management Grade Scale-I officer in PSU banks (IBPS — 11 PSBs)
- **Topic taxonomy:** English (RC, cloze, error spotting, para jumbles, sentence improvement, fillers, vocab); QA (simplification, number series, quadratic equations, DI — bar/line/pie/caselet, arithmetic — percentage, ratio, average, profit/loss, SI/CI, time-speed-distance, time-work, mixtures, partnership, mensuration); Reasoning (puzzles, seating arrangement — linear/circular/floor, syllogism, blood relations, direction sense, coding-decoding, inequalities, input-output, data sufficiency, logical reasoning); General/Banking Awareness (banking terms, RBI, monetary policy, financial sector, government schemes, current affairs); Computer Aptitude (basics, MS Office, internet, networking, security)

#### 30. IBPS Clerk / SBI Clerk
- **Conducting body:** IBPS / SBI
- **Eligibility:** Graduate; age 20–28
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:** Prelims (English, Numerical Ability, Reasoning Ability); Mains (General/Financial Awareness, General English, Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude, Quantitative Aptitude)
- **Annual aspirants:** IBPS Clerk ~15–20 lakh; SBI Clerk ~20–25 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Clerk/Junior Associate in PSU banks
- **Topic taxonomy:** English, QA, Reasoning, GA + Computer (similar topic list to PO but lower difficulty)

#### 31. RBI Grade B Officer
- **Conducting body:** Reserve Bank of India
- **Eligibility:** Graduate with ≥60% (relaxation for SC/ST/PwBD); age 21–30
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:**
  - **Phase 1:** General Awareness, English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning
  - **Phase 2:** Paper-I Economic & Social Issues, Paper-II English (descriptive), Paper-III Finance & Management
  - Interview
- **Annual aspirants:** ~2–3 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Manager (Grade B) — General/DEPR/DSIM
- **Topic taxonomy:** Phase-1 standard (GA, Eng, QA, Reasoning); Phase-2 ESI (growth & development, Indian economy, social structure, poverty, inequality, demographic trends, globalisation); FM (financial system, financial markets, risk management, basics of derivatives, banking regulations; management — leadership, motivation, organisational behaviour, HRD, corporate governance, ethics)

#### 32. NABARD Grade A/B Officer
- **Conducting body:** National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development
- **Eligibility:** Graduate (specialist disciplines for some posts)
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:** Phase 1 (Reasoning, English, Computer, QA, GA, Economic & Social Issues, Agriculture & Rural Development); Phase 2 (Descriptive — General English, ESI + ARD, optional)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~1.5–2 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Assistant Manager in NABARD
- **Topic taxonomy:** Standard banking subjects + ESI + ARD (rural economy, agricultural finance, rural credit, cooperative movement, agricultural marketing, government schemes for rural development)

#### 33. LIC AAO / ADO
- **Conducting body:** Life Insurance Corporation of India
- **Eligibility:** Graduate; age 21–30
- **Frequency:** As per vacancy notification
- **Sections:** Prelims (Reasoning, QA, English); Mains (Reasoning, GK & Current Affairs, Data Analysis & Interpretation, Insurance & Financial Market Awareness, English descriptive)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~5–8 lakh
- **Career outcome:** Assistant Administrative Officer / Apprentice Development Officer at LIC
- **Topic taxonomy:** Reasoning, QA, English, GK/Current Affairs, Insurance & Financial Awareness

### Category 7: SSC / Railway

#### 34. SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level)
- **Conducting body:** Staff Selection Commission
- **Eligibility:** Graduate; age 18/20–27/30/32 (post-dependent)
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Sections:**
  - **Tier 1:** General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension (25 Q each, 200 marks total)
  - **Tier 2:** Paper-I (Math, Reasoning & GI, English & Comprehension, GA, Computer Knowledge, Skill Test/Typing); Paper-II (Statistics — for JSO); Paper-III (Finance & Economics — for AAO)
- **Annual aspirants:** ~25–35 lakh

- **Geography:** Fundamentals of Human Geography; India — People & Economy; Practical Work in Geography
- **Psychology:** Variations in Psychological Attributes; Self & Personality; Meeting Life Challenges; Psychological Disorders; Therapeutic Approaches; Attitude & Social Cognition; Social Influence & Group Processes; Psychology & Life
- **Sociology:** Indian Society (Demographic Structure, Cultural Diversity, Social Institutions, Social Inequality & Exclusion, Social Movements); Social Change & Development in India (Structural Change, Cultural Change, Story of Indian Democracy, Change & Development in Rural/Urban/Industrial Society, Globalisation, Mass Media)
- **Computer Science (Python):** Computational Thinking & Programming (Python — functions, exception handling, file handling, data structures); Computer Networks; Database Management (SQL)

### ICSE / ISC (Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations)

**ICSE (Class 9–10):** Group I (compulsory) — English, Second Language (Hindi/regional/modern foreign language); Group II (any 2–3) — History/Civics/Geography (compulsory), Mathematics, Science (Phy/Chem/Bio); Group III (any 1–2 electives) — Computer Applications, Economics, Commercial Studies, Environmental Applications, Art, Performing Arts, Home Science, Cookery, Fashion Designing, Physical Education, Yoga, Technical Drawing Applications, Modern Foreign Language

**ISC (Class 11–12):** English compulsory + 3, 4, or 5 electives (max 6 papers including English) from: Hindi/Indian Language; Modern Foreign Language; Classical Language; Elective English; Mathematics; Physics; Chemistry; Biology; Biotechnology; Environmental Science; Computer Science; Geography; History; Political Science; Sociology; Psychology; Economics; Commerce; Accounts; Business Studies; Legal Studies; Art; Geometrical & Mechanical Drawing; Geometrical & Building Drawing; Home Science; Fashion Designing; Electricity & Electronics; Physical Education; Music (Indian/Western)

**Stream archetypes (informal — students customise):**
- **Science:** English + Physics + Chemistry + Math/Bio + 1 elective (Computer Science / Psychology / Economics / etc.)
- **Commerce:** English + Accounts + Commerce + Economics + 1 elective (Math / Business Studies / Computer Science)
- **Humanities:** English + History + Political Science + Geography/Psychology/Sociology + 1 elective (Economics / Legal Studies / Art / etc.)

**ISC subject quirks vs CBSE:**
- ISC English has a separate Literature paper (Drama + Prose + Poetry from a prescribed reading list) — generally regarded as more rigorous than CBSE
- ISC has Project Work component in most subjects (20% of marks)
- Practical components more substantial in Sciences

### IB Diploma Programme (IBDP) — India

Six subject groups + 3 core elements (TOK, EE, CAS). Students take 3 HL + 3 SL (or 4 HL + 2 SL).

- **Group 1 (Studies in Language & Literature):** Language A: Literature; Language A: Language & Literature; Literature & Performance — offered in English, Hindi, French, Spanish, etc.
- **Group 2 (Language Acquisition):** Language B (intermediate); ab initio (beginner) — English B, French B, Spanish B, Hindi B, Mandarin ab initio, etc.
- **Group 3 (Individuals & Societies):** Business Management; Economics; Geography; Global Politics; History; Information Technology in a Global Society; Philosophy; Psychology; Social & Cultural Anthropology; World Religions; Environmental Systems & Societies (interdisciplinary, also Group 4)
- **Group 4 (Sciences):** Biology; Chemistry; Physics; Computer Science; Design Technology; Sports/Exercise/Health Science; Environmental Systems & Societies
- **Group 5 (Mathematics):** Mathematics: Analysis & Approaches (HL/SL); Mathematics: Applications & Interpretation (HL/SL)
- **Group 6 (The Arts):** Visual Arts; Music; Theatre; Dance; Film (Group 6 optional — can substitute another Group 1–5 subject)
- **Core:** Theory of Knowledge (TOK), Extended Essay (EE — 4,000-word independent research paper), CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service)

## Recent (2025–2026) Context Notes (for ScaleUp seeding decisions)

- **CUET expansion:** CUET UG is now the dominant pan-India UG entrance for university admissions, replacing CU/DU/JNU/BHU separate entrances. CUET PG follows similar trajectory. NTA in 2026 has revised syllabi for History, Economics, Business Studies, Computer Science, Physical Education domain papers.
- **NEP 2020 impact on 9–12:** CBSE has phased in competency-based assessment (case-based, application questions). Class 10 Math now has Standard vs Basic option. Class 11–12 has more flexibility in subject combinations under NCF-SE 2023.
- **Two-board-exam model (CBSE):** From academic year 2025–26 onwards, CBSE Class 10 Board exams are being conducted twice a year (best-of-two). Class 12 still single annual board.
- **JEE Advanced foreign student admission:** From 2025, JEE Advanced opened to foreign nationals for admission to IITs (separate quota).
- **GMAT Focus Edition:** Has fully replaced classic GMAT — no more AWA essay, no Sentence Correction; Data Insights replaces Integrated Reasoning.
- **CFA exam windows:** Level I now has 4 windows/year (Feb/May/Aug/Nov); Level II has 3; Level III has 2. CBT format throughout.
- **CA New Scheme (May 2024 onwards):** ICAI consolidated subjects — CA Final reduced from 8 to 6 papers; new "Integrated Business Solutions" multidisciplinary case study at Final.
- **NEET PG 2026:** Single-shift format restored; NBEMS continues with normalised scoring approach.
- **GATE 2026:** Total 30 papers; new XE-I (Energy Science) section added under Engineering Sciences. Data Science & AI (DA) paper continues to grow rapidly in popularity.
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA):** Replacing IPC/CrPC/IEA from July 2024. Law curricula and CLAT/AILET legal-reasoning passages now reference both old and new codes.
