# India Skills Research — 2026 and Beyond

**Compiled:** 2026-05-03
**Purpose:** Seed topic taxonomy for the ScaleUp platform — onboarding suggestions for working professionals and students in India.
**Lens:** India-first. Where global trends differ from India, India's reality wins.

---

## Headline signals (context for the list below)

- **AI hiring surge:** LinkedIn India reports ~60% YoY growth in AI-related hiring; Naukri JobSpeak shows AI/ML roles closing FY26 at +45% YoY.
- **Skills > degrees:** ~85% of Indian employers now prioritise skills over degrees in at least some hires (India Skills Report 2026 / HRKatha).
- **Employability gap:** 38% of Indian job seekers feel unprepared for current technology shifts; 74% of Indian recruiters report difficulty finding qualified talent (LinkedIn India 2026).
- **GCC tailwind:** India's Global Capability Centres employ ~1.9M+ professionals and are projected to add 4.25–4.5 lakh new jobs in FY26, concentrating demand in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Gurugram.
- **WEF baseline:** ~44% of core skills in most roles will change by 2026; analytical thinking, AI/ML application, and tech literacy top the global core-skills list, all confirmed for India.
- **India-specific compliance load:** 2026 is the execution year for the DPDP Act (Rules notified Nov 2025), the new RBI 2FA mandate (effective 1 April 2026), and continued GST + Ind AS / IFRS convergence — all driving niche compliance hiring.

## Section A: Top 30 High-Demand Skills in India 2026

Tiering: **Critical** (severe shortage + cross-sector demand) · **High** (strong demand, multiple sectors) · **Emerging** (rapidly growing, smaller current base).

### 1. Generative AI & LLM Application Building
- **Description:** Designing, integrating and shipping features built on large language models — RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, fine-tuning, evals, and production deployment of GenAI systems.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, Product, Analytics, Customer Support
- **Why in demand:** NASSCOM projects India will need 1M+ AI professionals by 2026; LinkedIn India saw ~60% YoY growth in AI hiring with "AI Engineer" and "Prompt Engineer" topping Jobs on the Rise.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 2. Prompt Engineering & AI Workflow Design
- **Description:** Crafting, structuring and stress-testing prompts; designing multi-step AI workflows; building reusable prompt libraries and evaluation harnesses across functions.
- **Primary domains:** All knowledge work (Tech, Marketing, Product, HR, Sales, Operations)
- **Why in demand:** LinkedIn India lists prompt engineering and visual storytelling as among the fastest-growing skills in 2026; OpenAI and Anthropic role-specific prompt packs are now a standard onboarding artefact in Indian GCCs.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 3. Cloud Architecture (AWS / Azure / GCP)
- **Description:** Designing, deploying and operating workloads on hyperscaler clouds — networking, IAM, multi-region resilience, cost optimisation, and migration patterns.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, BFSI, GCC, Telecom
- **Why in demand:** NASSCOM estimates cloud will account for ~8% of India's GDP and create ~1.4 crore direct/indirect jobs by 2026; Cloud Architect is consistently in the top 5 GCC roles.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 4. Cybersecurity Operations & SOC Engineering
- **Description:** Threat detection, incident response, SIEM/SOAR tooling, vulnerability management, and 24x7 SOC operations aligned to RBI/CERT-In timelines.
- **Primary domains:** BFSI, IT Services, Healthcare, Public Sector
- **Why in demand:** India needs ~1M cybersecurity professionals against a current base of ~80K qualified experts (NASSCOM); RBI mandates 24x7 SOCs and 6-hour CERT-In incident reporting.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 5. Data Engineering (Spark, Snowflake, Databricks, dbt)
- **Description:** Building reliable batch and streaming pipelines, lakehouse architectures, and the data foundations that AI/analytics products depend on.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, BFSI, E-commerce, Telecom, Healthcare
- **Why in demand:** Naukri JobSpeak: Data Platform Specialists +28%, Big Data Testing Engineers +26% YoY in early 2026; Bengaluru data engineer salaries ₹15–30 LPA, scaling to ₹50 LPA at senior levels.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 6. Applied Machine Learning & MLOps
- **Description:** Training, deploying and monitoring ML models in production — feature stores, model registries, drift detection, and CI/CD for ML.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, BFSI, Healthtech, Logistics
- **Why in demand:** Indian fintechs (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cred) and BFSI GCCs treat MLOps as table-stakes for fraud, underwriting and personalisation systems.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 7. Full-Stack Software Development (MERN / Java Spring Boot + React + AWS)
- **Description:** End-to-end web/mobile development — frontend (React/Next.js), backend (Node.js, Spring Boot, Django), databases, REST/GraphQL APIs, and basic DevOps.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, SaaS, GCC, Startups
- **Why in demand:** "Full Stack Engineer" remains the highest-volume role on Naukri across IT services and Indian SaaS; MERN+AWS+TypeScript and Java Spring Boot+React are the highest-paying combos for freshers in FY26.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 8. DevOps, SRE & Platform Engineering
- **Description:** CI/CD, Kubernetes, infrastructure-as-code (Terraform), observability, and internal developer platforms that let product teams ship safely at scale.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, GCC, BFSI
- **Why in demand:** Persol India and JLL list Platform Engineering and SRE among the fastest-expanding GCC functions in 2026; every scaled Indian startup (Zomato, Razorpay, Meesho) runs a platform team.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 9. Product Management (B2B SaaS & AI Products)
- **Description:** Discovery, prioritisation, roadmapping, GTM, and AI-product specifics — model selection, evals, hallucination handling, and AI UX patterns.
- **Primary domains:** SaaS, FinTech, Consumer Internet, Healthtech
- **Why in demand:** Product hiring in India grew ~42% YoY in 2026, with AI-PM skills commanding a 20–50% premium; senior PM packages reach ₹65 LPA+.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 10. UX / Product Design (Figma + AI Product Design)
- **Description:** Research, IA, interaction design, prototyping in Figma, and designing AI-native experiences (chat, agents, generative interfaces).
- **Primary domains:** SaaS, Consumer Internet, FinTech
- **Why in demand:** Figma's 2026 designer survey found 82% of design leaders reported flat-or-rising headcount; 73% of Indian hiring managers now require AI-tool fluency in designers.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 11. Performance Marketing (Google + Meta Ads + Attribution)
- **Description:** Paid media strategy, campaign management, conversion-rate optimisation, and ROI/attribution measurement across Google, Meta, and emerging CTV channels.
- **Primary domains:** D2C, E-commerce, EdTech, FinTech
- **Why in demand:** India digital ad market projected at $35B by 2030 (29% CAGR); Performance Marketing Managers in India earn ₹12–25L; 45% of employers report increasing difficulty hiring skilled performance marketers (GROWAI / Digitalwala).
- **Demand tier:** High

### 12. SEO & AI-Search Optimisation (GEO/AEO)
- **Description:** Technical SEO, content strategy, and the new discipline of optimising for generative AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
- **Primary domains:** D2C, B2B SaaS, Media, EdTech
- **Why in demand:** As AI Overviews cannibalise traditional SERPs, Indian D2C brands and SaaS companies are urgently hiring "GEO/AEO specialists" — a brand-new sub-discipline in 2026.
- **Demand tier:** Emerging

### 13. Data Analytics & SQL Storytelling
- **Description:** SQL, Python (pandas), BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker), and the ability to translate data into decisions for non-technical stakeholders.
- **Primary domains:** All (BFSI, Consulting, Product, Marketing, Ops)
- **Why in demand:** "Data storytelling" and "data reporting" both made LinkedIn India's top 10 emerging IT skills for 2026; analytics is the most-requested cross-functional skill in GCC job descriptions.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 14. AI Product Engineering (RAG, Agents, Vector DBs)
- **Description:** Building production AI systems — retrieval-augmented generation, agentic frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI), vector databases (Pinecone, pgvector), and tool-use orchestration.
- **Primary domains:** Tech, Enterprise SaaS, BFSI
- **Why in demand:** Indian SaaS (Freshworks, Zoho, Postman, ChargeBee) and Indian-built AI startups (Sarvam, Krutrim, CoRover) are hiring aggressively for production-grade GenAI engineering.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 15. Semiconductor / VLSI Design (RTL, Physical Design, Verification)
- **Description:** RTL coding, verification (UVM/SystemVerilog), physical design at 5nm/3nm, DFT, and analog/mixed-signal design.
- **Primary domains:** Semiconductors, Consumer Electronics, Auto
- **Why in demand:** India Semiconductor Mission targets ~10 lakh new jobs by 2026; Tata fab in Dholera, Micron Sanand, and >20 fabless design centres (Qualcomm, NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Samsung, plus Indian startups like Saankhya Labs) are hiring at scale. Salaries ₹4–60 LPA across experience.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 16. FP&A and Corporate Finance Modelling
- **Description:** Three-statement modelling, scenario planning, variance analysis, and partnering with business units to drive financial decisions.
- **Primary domains:** GCC, BFSI, Consulting, SaaS
- **Why in demand:** FP&A is the #1 hiring function in Indian GCCs by volume in FY25–FY26 (Alysavision); MNCs centralise global FP&A in India because of the deep CA + Excel + analytics talent pool.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 17. IFRS / Ind AS Reporting
- **Description:** Applying IFRS / Ind AS standards for consolidation, revenue recognition (Ind AS 115), leases (Ind AS 116), and financial-instrument accounting.
- **Primary domains:** GCC, Listed Cos, Big 4, BFSI
- **Why in demand:** India's continued Ind AS / IFRS convergence and the rise of Indian-listed entities operating globally drives steady demand; IFRS-skilled CAs in Mumbai/Bengaluru earn ₹17–23 LPA (Saraf Academy / Strideedutech).
- **Demand tier:** High

### 18. GST, Indian Tax & Regulatory Advisory
- **Description:** GST compliance, e-invoicing, transfer pricing, income-tax advisory, startup-CFO services, and navigating evolving CBIC notifications.
- **Primary domains:** Big 4, Boutique CA practices, Indian corporates, Startups
- **Why in demand:** GST is uniquely Indian — no global skill substitutes. CAs specialising in GST, startup CFO, and tax tech build highly profitable practices; demand is structural and growing with each Finance Bill.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 19. RBI / SEBI / DPDP Compliance & Data Privacy (DPO)
- **Description:** Implementing the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, RBI cybersecurity / outsourcing / payment regulations, and SEBI listing/disclosure norms.
- **Primary domains:** BFSI, FinTech, Listed Cos, Health-tech
- **Why in demand:** 2026 is the DPDP execution year (Rules notified Nov 2025) and RBI's 2FA mandate goes live 1 April 2026; every regulated entity needs Data Protection Officers and compliance analysts. UpGuard / PwC India confirm a "race to readiness."
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 20. Enterprise B2B Sales & Account Management
- **Description:** Consultative selling, MEDDIC/MEDDPICC, multi-stakeholder enterprise deal cycles, RevOps fluency, and CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) discipline.
- **Primary domains:** SaaS, IT Services, BFSI, Industrial
- **Why in demand:** Indian SaaS expansion into US/EMEA + GCC client-facing teams have created a structural shortage of enterprise-sales leaders; mid-level enterprise AMs ₹18–40 LPA, Directors ₹60L–₹1.5Cr+ (Taggd 2026).
- **Demand tier:** High

### 21. Sustainability, ESG Reporting & Climate Risk
- **Description:** BRSR (Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting), GHG accounting, climate-risk modelling, and ESG due-diligence for investors and regulators.
- **Primary domains:** Listed Cos, BFSI, Manufacturing, Consulting
- **Why in demand:** SEBI's BRSR Core mandate covers India's top 1,000 listed entities; the green-talent gap in India is 1.5–2M against a 7.29M projected green-jobs base by FY28 (Taggd / Sustainability17). Compensation rising 12–30%.
- **Demand tier:** Emerging

### 22. EV, Battery & Renewable Energy Engineering
- **Description:** Battery cell design, BMS, powertrain engineering, solar/wind project execution, and green-hydrogen systems.
- **Primary domains:** Auto, Energy, Industrial
- **Why in demand:** PLI for Auto/ACC, India's National Green Hydrogen Mission, and Tata/Reliance/Adani battery investments are creating roles faster than the talent base can fill them.
- **Demand tier:** Emerging

### 23. Stakeholder Communication & Executive Storytelling
- **Description:** Structuring written and verbal updates for senior audiences — exec memos, board decks, async updates, and cross-cultural communication with global stakeholders.
- **Primary domains:** All (especially GCC, Consulting, Product, Sales)
- **Why in demand:** WEF FoJ 2025: 3 of the top 5 employer-demanded skills are soft skills; India's GCCs explicitly cite written-communication weakness as the #1 promotion blocker for mid-level talent.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 24. Critical Thinking & Structured Problem-Solving
- **Description:** Hypothesis-driven thinking, root-cause analysis, MECE structuring, and the ability to break ambiguous problems into solvable parts.
- **Primary domains:** Consulting, Product, Strategy, Engineering
- **Why in demand:** Analytical thinking is WEF's #1 core skill globally and in India for 2025–2030; this is the skill consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) and Indian product orgs screen for hardest.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 25. Adaptability & Continuous Learning
- **Description:** Self-directed learning, navigating ambiguity, and rapid skill pivots as tools/AI shift quarterly.
- **Primary domains:** All
- **Why in demand:** WEF says ~44% of core skills will change by 2026; India Skills Report 2026 names "learning agility" the single biggest predictor of internal promotion in Indian GCCs.
- **Demand tier:** Critical

### 26. Leadership & People Management (First-time Manager skills)
- **Description:** 1:1s, feedback delivery, performance management, hiring, and leading hybrid/distributed teams.
- **Primary domains:** All
- **Why in demand:** India's IT and SaaS scale-ups create thousands of first-time managers each year; Deloitte's 2026 Human Capital Trends names "leader development" the #1 unmet HR priority in India.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 27. Workforce/People Analytics & HR Tech
- **Description:** People-data modelling, attrition prediction, DEI analytics, and operating modern HR tech stacks (Workday, Darwinbox, Keka).
- **Primary domains:** HR, GCC, Consulting
- **Why in demand:** AIHR / Radancy 2026: predictive HR analytics becomes "operationally credible" in 2026; Indian GCCs are the global delivery centres for people-analytics work.
- **Demand tier:** Emerging

### 28. Customer Success & Retention (CS Ops)
- **Description:** Onboarding, account health scoring, NRR/GRR optimisation, churn prediction, and renewal/expansion playbooks.
- **Primary domains:** B2B SaaS, FinTech, EdTech
- **Why in demand:** Indian SaaS (Freshworks, Zoho, Chargebee, Atlan) and global GCC CS teams are doubling CS headcount as NRR becomes the leading SaaS valuation metric.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 29. Project & Program Management (Agile + PMP)
- **Description:** Agile/Scrum delivery, large-program governance (PMP/PRINCE2), risk and dependency management across multi-team initiatives.
- **Primary domains:** IT Services, GCC, BFSI, Manufacturing
- **Why in demand:** Indian IT services ($250B+ exports) and GCC programmes still run on disciplined PMs; Agile coaching and SAFe-trained leads remain consistently in shortage.
- **Demand tier:** High

### 30. Vernacular / Bharat-Market Content & Voice AI
- **Description:** Designing content, UX and conversational AI in Indian languages — Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi — for Tier-2/3 users; speech recognition and TTS in Indic languages.
- **Primary domains:** Consumer Internet, FinTech, EdTech, GovTech
- **Why in demand:** ONDC, account-aggregator, Bhashini and the next 500M Indian internet users live in vernacular; Sarvam, Krutrim, CoRover and consumer apps (PhonePe, ShareChat) are hiring Indic-NLP, Indic-content and vernacular-PM roles.
- **Demand tier:** Emerging

## Section B: AI Literacy per Domain

For each domain, 3–5 specific AI tools/skills professionals should learn in 2026.

### 1. Product Management
1. **Prompt engineering for product specs** — Drafting and stress-testing PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria with structured prompts. (Tools: Claude, ChatGPT, ChatPRD)
2. **AI-assisted user-research synthesis** — Clustering interview transcripts, extracting jobs-to-be-done, and surfacing insight themes. (Tools: Claude Projects, Dovetail AI, Notion AI)
3. **AI roadmapping & meeting intelligence** — Auto-summarising stakeholder meetings, generating retro notes, and triaging feedback into Jira/Linear. (Tools: Fireflies, Loom AI, Linear AI, Jira AI)
4. **Building lightweight AI agents for PM workflows** — Scoped agents that monitor metrics, summarise CSAT, or draft release notes. (Tools: Claude Agent SDK, n8n, Zapier AI)
5. **Evaluating AI features (evals & guardrails)** — Designing evals for hallucination, latency, and quality before shipping AI features. (Tools: Braintrust, LangSmith, Promptfoo)

### 2. Software Engineering
1. **Agentic coding in the IDE** — Multi-file edits, planning, and refactors with autonomy controls. (Tools: Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot)
2. **AI-driven code review & test generation** — Writing unit/integration tests, reviewing PRs, and catching regressions. (Tools: GitHub Copilot Review, CodeRabbit, Cursor agents)
3. **RAG and vector-DB integration** — Building retrieval pipelines on company data for internal search and copilots. (Tools: Pinecone, pgvector, LangChain, LlamaIndex)
4. **AI debugging & log triage** — Pasting stack traces and logs into LLMs for root-cause and fix suggestions. (Tools: Claude, ChatGPT, Sentry AI)
5. **Spec-to-code prototyping** — Going from a Figma file or Markdown spec to a working prototype. (Tools: Claude Code, v0.dev, Bolt, Lovable)

### 3. Data Science / Analytics
1. **Natural-language SQL & analyst copilots** — Talking to warehouses to draft and refine SQL. (Tools: Snowflake Cortex, Databricks Genie, Hex Magic)
2. **Notebook copilots for EDA & modelling** — Auto-suggesting transforms, charts, and model baselines. (Tools: Jupyter AI, Hex, Deepnote AI)
3. **AutoML & feature engineering assistants** — Bootstrapping baseline models and feature pipelines. (Tools: Databricks AutoML, Vertex AI, H2O)
4. **LLM-powered dashboards & narrative analytics** — Auto-generating insight summaries for execs. (Tools: ThoughtSpot Sage, Power BI Copilot, Tableau Pulse)
5. **Embeddings & semantic search for analysts** — Clustering tickets, surveys, and free-text data. (Tools: OpenAI/Cohere embeddings, Pinecone)

### 4. Marketing (Digital + Performance)
1. **AI ad-creative generation & testing** — Generating image/video creative variants at campaign scale. (Tools: Meta Advantage+, Pencil, Adcreative.ai, Midjourney)
2. **Generative campaign copy & A/B variants** — Drafting ad headlines, email subject lines, landing-page copy. (Tools: Jasper, Copy.ai, ChatGPT)
3. **AI media-buying & budget optimisation** — Bid optimisation across Google/Meta and predictive LTV models. (Tools: Google Performance Max, Smartly, Northbeam)
4. **GEO/AEO — Generative Engine Optimisation** — Optimising content to surface in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. (Tools: Profound, Otterly, Semrush AI)
5. **AI-powered MMM & attribution** — Modern marketing-mix modelling on first-party data. (Tools: Mass Analytics, Lifesight, Robyn)

### 5. Design (UX/UI + Visual)
1. **AI-assisted design generation in Figma** — Generating components, variants, and copy in-canvas. (Tools: Figma AI, Figma Make, Magician)
2. **Image/video generation for visual design** — Concepting illustrations, lifestyle imagery, and motion. (Tools: Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Runway)
3. **AI user-research synthesis** — Coding interviews and clustering insights from research repos. (Tools: Dovetail AI, Maze AI, UserTesting AI)
4. **Design-to-code handoff with AI** — Producing accurate, accessible code from Figma frames. (Tools: Figma Dev Mode + MCP, v0.dev, Anima)
5. **Designing AI-native UX patterns** — Chat, agent, streaming, hallucination-handling and trust UX. (Reference: Anthropic, OpenAI, Vercel AI SDK design guides)

### 6. Sales
1. **AI sales-call intelligence** — Auto-transcribing calls, extracting next steps, and coaching reps. (Tools: Gong, Chorus, Fathom)
2. **Personalised outbound at scale** — Researching prospects and drafting tailored emails. (Tools: Clay, Apollo AI, Lemlist AI)
3. **CRM AI copilots** — Pipeline hygiene, deal-risk scoring, and forecast intelligence. (Tools: Salesforce Einstein, HubSpot Breeze)
4. **AI proposal & RFP generation** — Auto-drafting RFP responses against your knowledge base. (Tools: Loopio AI, Qwilr AI, Claude)
5. **Conversational discovery prep** — Using LLMs to research accounts and pre-build MEDDIC/MEDDPICC qualification. (Tools: Claude Projects, ChatGPT with web)

### 7. HR / People Ops
1. **AI-assisted JD writing & sourcing** — Inclusive JDs, boolean strings, and outreach personalisation. (Tools: Textio, LinkedIn Recruiter AI, hireEZ)
2. **AI-screening & structured-interview support** — Resume screening, interview-question generation, scorecards. (Tools: Eightfold, HireVue, Metaview)
3. **People-analytics copilots** — Attrition prediction, DEI analytics, engagement insights. (Tools: Visier, Workday Illuminate, Darwinbox AI)
4. **AI for L&D content & coaching** — Personalised learning paths, AI role-play coaching. (Tools: Sana, Docebo AI, BetterUp AI)
5. **Policy/handbook chatbots (HR helpdesk)** — RAG over HR policies for employee self-service. (Tools: Glean, Moveworks, Leena AI — Indian)

### 8. Finance / Accounting
1. **AI document extraction (invoices, contracts, bank statements)** — Auto-extracting structured data from finance docs. (Tools: Rossum, Hyperscience, Klippa, Vic.ai)
2. **AI-powered FP&A & narrative reporting** — Variance commentary, budget drafts, and board-deck narratives. (Tools: Datarails FP&A Genius, Pigment AI, Mosaic)
3. **AI tax & GST research** — Querying GST notifications, case law, and Income-tax sections in natural language. (Tools: Taxmann AI, Cleartax, ChatGPT with retrieval)
4. **AI audit & controls testing** — Sampling, anomaly detection, and journal-entry testing. (Tools: MindBridge, Caseware AI, Auditboard AI)
5. **Excel + AI copilots** — Formulas, model audit, and scenario generation directly in spreadsheets. (Tools: Excel Copilot, Numerous.ai, GPT for Sheets)

### 9. Consulting
1. **AI-driven secondary research & market sizing** — Synthesising reports, building market maps, generating TAM models. (Tools: Perplexity Pro, Claude with web, AlphaSense)
2. **Slide generation & deck assembly** — Drafting slides, charts, and exhibit narratives. (Tools: Gamma, Tome, Beautiful.ai, Presentations.AI)
3. **Expert-call & interview synthesis** — Clustering 50+ expert interviews into themes. (Tools: Otter, Fireflies, Claude Projects)
4. **AI-assisted client-deliverable drafting** — Working drafts of strategy memos, diagnostics, and PoVs. (Tools: Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise)
5. **Custom GPTs / Claude Projects for engagements** — Scoped knowledge bases per client, embedding methodologies and frameworks. (Tools: ChatGPT Custom GPTs, Claude Projects)

### 10. Founder / Entrepreneurship
1. **End-to-end AI coding for MVPs** — Shipping a working product without a full eng team. (Tools: Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, v0)
2. **AI for fundraising & investor comms** — Pitch-deck drafts, financial-model scaffolds, investor-update generation. (Tools: Claude, Gamma, Pitch, ChatGPT)
3. **AI for content & founder-led marketing** — Long-form posts, video scripts, podcast clips at solo-founder scale. (Tools: Descript, Opus Clip, Riverside AI, ChatGPT)
4. **AI customer-support agents (early-stage)** — Cheap, 24x7 first-line support for Bharat customers. (Tools: Intercom Fin, CoRover, Yellow.ai — Indian, Crisp AI)
5. **AI ops & back-office automation** — Email triage, scheduling, accounting prep, recruiting. (Tools: Zapier AI, n8n, Lindy, Notion AI, Tally + AI extensions)
